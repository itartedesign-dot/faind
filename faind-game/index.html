      About News Rush

      News Rush is a free browser arcade game in the style of the 80s and 90s cabinets, drawn in pixel art, with no download or sign-up. Set a high score and see where you rank on the online leaderboard.

      You play Faindo, the FAIND mascot. News scrolls fall into the newsroom and you have to catch them before they hit the floor. The levels get faster, and the leaderboard resets every week next to an all-time ranking.

      Controls: left and right arrow keys or A and D on a keyboard, drag or the on-screen arrows on a phone. P or Esc pauses.

      The game was designed and built by FAIND with the help of artificial intelligence.
