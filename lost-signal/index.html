      About Lost Signal

      A memory game that will make you hold your breath. Lost Signal is a free pixel-art memory game with a retro arcade feel. It runs in the browser, with no download or sign-up, and has an online leaderboard for the best scores.

      Every level shows a path of floating tiles for seven seconds, then the lights go out. You walk and jump the path from memory to reach the newsroom. You have 3 lives and 20 flashes to reveal the path again, and there is no time limit. 50 levels.

      Controls: arrow keys or WASD to walk, Shift plus a direction to turn, Space to jump, F for a flash, P to pause. On a phone, swipe to walk and tap beside Faindo to turn.

      The game was designed and built by FAIND with the help of artificial intelligence.
