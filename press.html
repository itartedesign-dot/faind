Facts

  • Name: FAIND – Flash AI News Daily (faind.org)
  • What it is: an independent news site about artificial intelligence.
  • How it works: every hour it collects stories from 34 news outlets and official AI company blogs, plus 11 YouTube channels. Each story keeps the name and link of the original source. When several outlets cover the same story, FAIND groups them and shows who reports it.
  • Languages: Italian, English, French, German.
  • Editorial pieces: in-depth answers, a glossary, topic pages, a price comparison of AI assistants and a daily editorial take (Il punto), each with author, update date and sources.
  • Also free on the site: RSS feeds, a Telegram channel (@faindnews), an embeddable news widget and a set of retro arcade games.
  • Business model: none so far. No advertising, no sign-up, no sponsored content. Voluntary donations only.
  • Founder and editor: Paolo Buono, Caltanissetta, Sicily, Italy.

Description to copy

One line:

FAIND is an independent site that tracks AI news hour by hour and always shows the original source.

Short paragraph:

FAIND (faind.org) is an independent news site about artificial intelligence, created by Italian graphic designer Paolo Buono. Every hour it collects stories from international outlets, official AI company blogs and selected YouTube channels, groups the ones that cover the same event and links to the original article. It is free, has no advertising and no sign-up, and is available in Italian, English, French and German.

How to cite FAIND

  • News stories: FAIND does not rewrite articles. Cite the original outlet. Cite FAIND when you refer to the multi-source view of a story.
  • Editorial pieces (in-depth articles, glossary, comparison, Il punto): cite FAIND with the page title, the date shown on the page and the URL.
  • Prices: the comparison shows the date each price was last checked against the official pricing page. Quote that date.
  • Suggested link text: FAIND, or FAIND – Flash AI News Daily. Link to https://faind.org/ or to the specific page.
