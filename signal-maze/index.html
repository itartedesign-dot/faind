      About Signal Maze

      Signal Maze is a free top-down maze game inspired by 80s arcade classics, with pixel-art graphics and a tense run: 50 levels in 35 minutes. Your score goes on an online leaderboard, so every run is a chance to beat the high score.

      Each level has coloured keys, documents and offices. Pick up a key, collect the document of the same colour and deliver it to the matching office, while patrolling robots hunt you through the corridors. If you lose all your lives you can continue from the game over screen.

      Controls: arrow keys or WASD to move, P or Esc to pause. On a phone, swipe to move.

      The game was designed and built by FAIND with the help of artificial intelligence.
