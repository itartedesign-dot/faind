    About Faind Games

    Faind Games is a small collection of free browser arcade games with the look and the pace of 80s and 90s arcades: pixel-art graphics, short runs, a high score to beat. They run on phone, tablet and computer, with no download and no account.

    They are original games, designed and built by FAIND with the help of artificial intelligence. Each game has its own online leaderboard, so you can compare your high score with players from other countries, and the three games share the same hero, Faindo, the mascot of the AI news site FAIND.
